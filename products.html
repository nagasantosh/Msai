What is Graphene?

Graphene is a single layer of carbon atoms arranged in a two-dimensional honeycomb lattice. It is the strongest material ever tested, with remarkable properties that revolutionize construction when integrated into concrete and other building materials.

Adding just 0.03-0.1% graphene to concrete delivers revolutionary improvements in strength, durability, and sustainability, making it a game-changer for modern construction projects.

OUR STORY

Building the Future Through Excellence & Innovation

From a single RMC plant in 2016 to a comprehensive solutions provider serving India, UAE, and the Middle East.

Our Journey

Mother Sai Group began its journey in 2016 with a single Ready Mix Concrete Plant in Tamil Nadu. Known for its unwavering commitment to quality and consistency, Mother Sai Group quickly earned the trust of customers and expanded to three RMC plants, while also extending consultancy to six more in the region.

From Humble Beginnings to Industry Leadership

Our strong customer focus and proven expertise helped us successfully penetrate the home market. Over time, the group has grown beyond concrete to offer plumbing and firefighting solutions, making it a comprehensive solutions provider for construction and infrastructure projects.

Today, we stand as a trusted partner to leading organizations including ISRO, L&T, JSW, Tata Power Solar, and many others, delivering excellence across every project we undertake.
